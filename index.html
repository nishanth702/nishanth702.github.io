About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at VIT Vellore, currently maintaining an 8.49 CGPA, with a genuine soft spot for the parts of CS that make everything else work: operating systems, database management, and increasingly, artificial intelligence. I like understanding systems from the inside out — how a scheduler picks its next process, how a query planner chooses an index, how a model actually learns.

That curiosity carries into what I build. I've shipped cloud-hosted tools on AWS, NLP pipelines for text classification, and small web platforms aimed at solving real, unglamorous problems — like connecting farmers directly with buyers. I care about writing software that's scalable, sensible, and doesn't fall over when someone other than me uses it.

Outside of code, I'm usually organizing something. And when I'm not doing either of those, I'm slowly working my way through Spanish — mostly for the joy of being bad at something new.
